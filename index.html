Sea Level Zero makes short videos that flood real cities with real data: Copernicus Sentinel-2 satellite images and Copernicus elevation data, with the sea raised metre by metre. A simulation, not a forecast.

The videos are published on our own YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat accounts by a small private tool that uses each platform's official API. The tool is used only by Sea Level Zero, for its own accounts.
